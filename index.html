fluid-surface

A fluid deformable surface: Krause & Voigt's perturbed sphere, r = 1 + r0 cos(phi) sin(3 theta), released at rest, with surface viscosity (Re 1), bending (Be 2) and its enclosed volume held fixed. Every step is MATLAB (models/stokes.m and lib/) compiled to WebGPU by numbl, solved by GMRES. A port of Dan Fortunato's C++ solver; BDF-1 with a fixed time step.
